ChatGPT ImageGen

ChatGPT ImageGen is surprisingly good for this task when you give it enough reference photos and the right example or prompt.

If you already pay for ChatGPT, it's definitely worth using.

If you're only buying a membership for this one job, though, I'd still go with Higgsfield, because the Soul 2.0 outputs are generally stronger and more consistent for realistic dating photos.

What You Need

  • 5–20 reference photos of yourself.
  • A ChatGPT membership, or around $20 to buy one.
  • One of the prompts or exemplar images from this guide.

Step-by-Step

1. Go to ChatGPT

Open the ChatGPT website or app and start a new chat.

2. Upload Your Reference Photos

Upload your 5–20 photos into the same conversation.

Try to give it a decent mix of:

  • Front-facing shots
  • Different angles
  • Smiling and neutral expressions
  • Upper-body shots
  • Full-body shots

The more information it has about what you actually look like, the easier it is for it to keep your face and body consistent.

3. Tell ChatGPT to Use Those Photos as Your Reference

Something simple is enough:

Use these uploaded images as the reference for my face, body, hair, and overall appearance in the images we create in this chat.

4. Give It the Image You Want to Create

You can either upload one of the exemplar images from this guide and ask it to place you into that type of shot, or paste one of the prompts from the prompt library.

Open the good-photo examples

Open the six-photo prompt library

Important: Your result can improve significantly when you attach a good example image alongside the prompt. The example gives ChatGPT the framing, setting, and overall feel to aim for; your reference photos tell it who you are.

The exemplar method is especially useful if you already like the pose, framing, setting, or general feel of an image and just want your character put into that style.

5. Generate the Image

Ask ChatGPT to create it using your uploaded photos as the character reference.

You'll normally get the image directly back in the chat.

6. Check the Result

If it looks right, save it and move on to the next one.

If something is off, keep the image and tell ChatGPT exactly what needs changing.

For example:

Keep everything else the same, but make my face closer to the reference photos.

Keep the image the same, but make me smile.

Keep the pose and background exactly the same, but change the jumper to a black T-shirt and chain.

Keep everything else unchanged, but make the lighting more realistic.

Use the same image, but have me looking at the camera.

7. Fix It Rather Than Starting Over

ChatGPT is particularly useful here because you can keep talking to it about the same image.

If the first output is 90% there, just correct the 10% that's wrong.

Usually, one or two follow-ups get you where you want.

8. If the Image Is Way Off, Regenerate It

If the face is wrong, the setting looks fake, or it just hasn't followed the example properly, don't waste time trying to rescue it.

Run the prompt again.

9. Save the Winner

Once you have something that passes, save it and move on to the next photo you need for your dating profile.

The ChatGPT Process

Upload your reference photos → give it the exemplar or prompt → generate → fix anything wrong → save the winner.

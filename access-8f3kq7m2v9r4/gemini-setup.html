GEMINI (Free & Fast)

What You Need

  • 5–10 photos of you and your body from different angles. The more variety, the better the realism.
  • A Gemini account.
  • Your dating-app profile.
  • A prompt or example image.

Step-by-Step

1. Go to Gemini.

2. Upload the 5–10 reference photos of your face and body into the same chat. Don't ask it to generate anything yet.

3. Tell Gemini that these are the reference photos it needs to use for your face, body, hair, and overall appearance.

4. Next, either upload one of the example photos from this guide:

Open the good-photo examples

Or paste in one of the prompts from the prompt library:

Open the six-photo prompt library

Important: Your result can improve significantly when you attach a good example image alongside the prompt. The example gives Gemini the framing, setting, and overall feel to aim for; your reference photos tell it who you are.

5. Generate the image.

6. If it comes out perfect, great. Save it and move on.

If it gets most of it right but messes up one or two things, don't start again straight away. Just tell it exactly what it got wrong.

For example:

> Keep everything else the same, but make my face look more like the reference photos.

> Same image, but make me smile.

> Keep everything the same, but change the jumper to a black T-shirt and chain.

> Keep the pose and setting, but make the photo look less AI and more like a normal phone photo.

Usually, one or two follow-up prompts are enough to get it where you want.

7. If the whole image is bad, regenerate it instead of wasting time trying to fix it.

8. Do a few versions of each photo. You're not trying to get every image perfect—you're trying to get one really good version for each slot in your profile.

9. Once you've got the winner, save it and move on to the next one.

That's the Process

Upload photos → give it the example or prompt → generate → fix what's wrong → save the best one.

# Higgsfield (More Expensive, Stronger Results)

## What You Need

- Your dating profile.
- Around $19 for the membership.
- 20+ photos of yourself from different angles for the best realism.

> **Note:** In my opinion, Higgsfield Soul gives the most realistic output of any AI model I tested.
>
> The downside is that it costs around $20 to get started.
>
> That's why we've included the free Gemini method as well. Gemini can still produce good, usable images, but if you want the strongest realism and consistency, Higgsfield is the better option.

## Step-by-Step

### 1. Go to Higgsfield

[https://higgsfield.ai/](https://higgsfield.ai/)

Sign in or create an account.

### 2. Create Your Character

Go to the **Characters** section and create a new Soul ID.

Upload 20+ photos of yourself if you can.

Try to give it a proper spread:

- Front-facing photos
- Side angles
- Smiling and neutral expressions
- Upper-body shots
- Full-body shots
- Different lighting and locations

You don't need professional photos. You just want enough variety for Higgsfield to properly understand what you look like.

The better the reference set, the better the likeness tends to be.

### 3. Let Higgsfield Build Your Character

Once your photos are uploaded, Higgsfield will create a reusable version of you.

This is the important difference compared with generating from one random selfie every time.

Once your character is built, you can keep putting the same version of yourself into different settings without having to upload all your photos again.

### 4. Use Soul 2.0 for the Actual Images

Go here:

[https://higgsfield.ai/ai/image?model=soul-v2](https://higgsfield.ai/ai/image?model=soul-v2)

Make sure **Soul 2.0** is selected.

This is the model I've found gives the best combination of:

- Realistic skin
- Believable lighting
- Natural-looking environments
- Good clothing
- Photos that actually feel like photographs rather than obvious AI images

### 5. Select Your Character

Choose the Soul ID you just created so Higgsfield knows who needs to appear in the image.

### 6. Choose the Type of Photo You Need

Look at your existing dating profile and decide which slot you're trying to improve.

For example:

- First photo
- Café shot
- Smart evening shot
- Dog photo
- Travel shot
- Outdoor/lifestyle shot

Then go to the matching prompt from the prompt library.

`[ATTACH PROMPT LIBRARY / IMAGES HERE]`

### 7. Paste the Prompt into Soul 2.0

Copy the prompt exactly and generate the image.

Because Higgsfield already knows what you look like through your Soul ID, you don't need to keep explaining your face every time.

The prompt is there to control:

- Setting
- Clothes
- Pose
- Expression
- Framing
- Lighting
- The overall feel of the photo

### 8. Generate the First Version

Higgsfield is generally much better at getting these right the first time than the free option.

A lot of the time, you should be able to get a usable result from the first generation.

If it looks good, save it.

### 9. If Something Is Wrong, Correct That Specific Thing

If the image is nearly right, don't throw the whole thing away.

Tell it what needs changing.

For example:

> Keep everything else exactly the same, but make me smile.

> Keep the same pose and setting, but make my face closer to the Soul ID.

> Keep everything the same, but change the shirt to a fitted black T-shirt and simple chain.

> Keep the character and pose, but make the lighting feel more like a normal phone photo.

> Keep everything else unchanged, but have me looking directly at the camera.

Usually, one correction is enough.

### 10. If the Generation Is Bad, Just Run It Again

Sometimes AI gives you a weird version for no real reason.

Don't spend ages fighting it.

Run the prompt again and compare the outputs.

You're trying to find the strongest photo, not fix every generation.

### 11. Save the Winner and Move On

Once you've got a photo you'd genuinely use on your profile, save it and move on to the next prompt.

Do the same process until you've replaced the weak photos in your dating profile.

## The Basic Higgsfield Process

Upload your photos → build Soul ID → use Soul 2.0 → paste prompt → generate → fix anything obvious → save the winner.

# How to Find More Good Photo Examples

If you cannot find an example photo in the gallery that feels right for you, Pinterest is an easy place to find more.

You are not looking for a perfect photo to copy exactly. You are looking for a pose, setting, outfit, or general feel that you would genuinely be happy to have on your profile.

## 1. Go to Pinterest

Start here: [Pinterest: man by the sea](https://uk.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=man%20by%20the%20sea&rs=typed)

This gives you a clear example of the kind of search you are making.

## 2. Search for the type of photo you want

Then search other concepts and find the examples you like enough to use for your own images. Keep the search simple. Try one of these:

- `man and dog portrait`
- `man coffee shop`
- `man cafe portrait`
- `man at pub`
- `man outdoors portrait`
- `man by the sea`
- `man sunset portrait`
- `man smart casual`
- `man walking dog`
- `man record shop`
- `man pottery`
- `man climbing`
- `man cooking`
- `man at market`
- `man travel portrait`

These are just a few examples. You can search for whatever you can think of.

## 3. Save a few examples you actually like

Look for photos you like, save them, then use them for future generations.

Save two or three examples for the photo slot you are working on. That is enough. You do not need to spend an hour collecting them.

## 4. Use the example with your prompt

When you create the image in ChatGPT or Gemini, upload:

1. Your own reference photos, so the AI knows what you look like.
2. The example image, so the AI understands the pose, setting, framing, and overall feel.
3. The prompt from the prompt library, so the AI knows exactly what to create.

Using a good example image alongside the prompt can improve the result a lot. The example shows the AI what you are aiming for; your reference photos keep the output looking like you.

Your Six-Photo Prompt Pack

Use these prompts once your AI image setup is ready. Paste one prompt into the AI, add your reference photo when it asks for one, and it will create an image for that part of your profile.

Work through the prompts until you have one image you genuinely like for each photo slot. You can also use a photo already in your camera roll if it is stronger and fits that slot better. This does not need to be perfect on the first try: generate a few versions, then keep the one that feels most like you.

Start with Photo 1. It is your hook photo: the clear, natural first picture that makes someone stop and look at the rest of your profile. Choose the version where your face is clearest, your expression feels natural, and you would be happy for it to be the first thing someone sees.

Photo 1 — Hook photo

Choose one of the prompts below. Each gives you a relaxed, clear café photo with a slightly different pose or setting.

Vertical 4:5 candid smartphone photo of {reference character} seated at an outdoor pavement table outside a smart but relaxed London café on a bright sunny afternoon. The café façade on camera-left is painted pale cream with large windowpanes; above, a striped cream-and-grey retractable awning creates soft diagonal lines. A hanging basket of trailing greenery sits above him. Behind him are occupied bistro tables, softly blurred diners, a tree-lined London street, red-brick Victorian mansion blocks, parked cars, a small traffic cone, and bright blue sky. Natural direct summer light with soft facial shadow, realistic iPhone camera sharpness and colour, ordinary but put-together, genuinely candid—not luxury advertising, not a fashion editorial, no heavy blur, no filter, no text overlay.

Photo 3 — Show personality

This is where someone gets a clearer sense of what you are like day to day. Choose one prompt that feels genuinely believable for you—dog if you have one, otherwise one of the coffee options.

Option 1: Dog — puppy at home

Vertical 4:5 candid smartphone photo of {reference character} standing in the open doorway of a modest, well-kept home in late-afternoon sun. Photograph from approximately 1 metre away at chest height, framing from his cap to just below his hips. He has a broad natural smile, stands relaxed while holding a golden retriever puppy securely across both arms, and wears a pale blue short-sleeve button-up shirt, a dark baseball cap, and no visible logos. The puppy is golden, floppy-eared, relaxed, and looking calmly toward the camera, with its front paws resting loosely over his arm.

The doorway is white-painted with simple raised panels and warm sunlight falling across it. Behind him is a dimmer room with dark blue walls, a window with closed blinds, and the edge of a sofa—ordinary, lived-in detail rather than a styled interior. His face and the puppy’s face are both clear and naturally lit. Simple friend-taken phone photo, warm and genuinely affectionate, realistic indoor-outdoor exposure, no novelty props, no heavy filter.

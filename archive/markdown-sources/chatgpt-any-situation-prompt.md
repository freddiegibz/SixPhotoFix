# The "What Do I Say Now?" ChatGPT Prompt

Use this when you have a real dating-app conversation in front of you and you are not sure what to say next.

Start with your goal. Then give ChatGPT the context. Then tell it what type of reply you need.

The more of the real conversation you include, the better the answer will be.

## Copy This Prompt

```text
You are helping me choose the next message in a dating-app conversation.

My goal is:
[Choose one: get a reply / keep the conversation interesting / bring back a chat that has gone quiet / ask her out / make a date plan / confirm or rearrange a date / get a second date / something else]

The situation:
[Explain what has happened in one or two sentences.]

Her profile:
[Paste her bio, prompts, and anything useful from her photos. If there is nothing useful, write "nothing to use".]

The conversation so far:
[Paste the messages, with her messages and my messages clearly labelled.]

What I want this reply to do:
[Choose one: make her curious / make her laugh or play along / keep the conversation moving / change the topic / ask for a date / get a clear answer / make a specific plan / restart the chat]

The tone I want:
[Choose one: natural / playful / confident / direct / lightly flirty]

Important rules:
- Write like a normal young man, not a dating coach or an AI.
- Use the real details from her profile and our conversation. Do not invent facts about her.
- Do not use pickup lines, long paragraphs, cheesy compliments, insults, sexual messages, or manipulative language.
- Keep the message short enough to send naturally.
- Do not make it sound overly polished or try-hard.
- If I am asking her out, make the suggestion clear and easy to answer.
- If she has already gone quiet or said she is busy, do not make me chase or pressure her.

Give me:
1. The best message to send
2. Two alternatives with slightly different tones
3. One sentence explaining why the best option fits this situation
4. The most likely ways she could reply, and what I should say next for each one
```

## Fully Worked Example: Asking Her Out

Here is what the prompt looks like when it is filled in properly.

```text
You are helping me choose the next message in a dating-app conversation.

My goal is:
ask her out

The situation:
We have had a good chat for a day. She is replying properly, asking me things back, and we have been joking about food. I want to ask her out without making it feel random.

Her profile:
- "My simple pleasures: trying every new pasta place in London"
- Photo holding a huge bowl of pasta in Italy
- Prompt: "I will fall for you if you can make me laugh"

The conversation so far:
Me: you have very strong pasta opinions for someone i have only just matched with
Her: as i should. bad pasta ruins my whole week
Me: bold claim. what counts as bad pasta then?
Her: anything with too much cream. it is lazy
Me: okay, slightly brutal but i respect it. i am more forgiving than you
Her: then you are part of the problem
Me: harsh. i feel like i need one chance to defend myself
Her: haha go on then

What I want this reply to do:
ask for a date that connects to the conversation we have been having

The tone I want:
playful and confident
```

A good ChatGPT answer would look like this:

```text
1. Best message

i think this pasta argument needs settling properly. let's get a drink this week and you can tell me where i have been going wrong

2. Alternatives

More direct:
you have made a convincing case that i need a proper pasta education. drink this week?

More playful:
i need one chance to defend my pasta standards in person. are you free for a drink this week?

3. Why it fits

It picks up the joke you already have, gives a clear reason to meet, and makes the date ask feel like the natural next part of the chat.

4. Likely replies and what to say next

If she says yes:
perfect. what day works best for you?

If she says she is busy but suggests another day:
no worries, [her suggested day] works. let's do it then.

If she says "maybe":
no stress. let me know if you decide you are up for it.

If she says she is busy without suggesting another day:
no worries, give me a shout when you have a gap.
```

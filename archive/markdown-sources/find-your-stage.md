# Find Your Stage

## 1. Get The Reply

You are here if you have matched, but have not sent the first message yet, or you sent one and she has not replied.

## 2. Build The Momentum

You are here if she is replying, but you are not sure how to keep the conversation interesting or move it beyond small talk.

## 3. Bring The Conversation Back

You are here if her replies have slowed down, become short, or stopped altogether.

## 4. Get The Yes

You are here if the conversation is going well and it is time to ask her out.

## 5. Put The Date In The Calendar

You are here if she has shown interest in meeting, but you do not yet have a specific day, time, and place agreed.

## 6. Make Sure She Shows Up

You are here if the date is booked and you want to confirm it, handle a cancellation, or rearrange without losing momentum.

## 7. Make The Date Feel Easy

You are here if the date is coming up and you want a clear plan for making it enjoyable, relaxed, and easy to continue.

## 8. Get The Second Date

You are here if the first date has happened and you want to see her again.

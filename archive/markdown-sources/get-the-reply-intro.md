# 1. Get The Reply

You have matched with her.

That means she is interested enough to see what you are about. This first message is the most important job in the whole conversation.

You just need to open it in a way that feels natural, gives her a reason to reply, and does not sound like the same thing every other guy on the app has sent.

Most guys get this wrong because they try to start a normal conversation straight away.

"Hey, how are you?"

"How has your week been?"

"What do you do for work?"

Nothing wrong with those questions in real life. But on a dating app, she has already seen them all. They do not make her feel anything. They do not give her a reason to choose your message over the next one.

Your first message has one job.

Make her curious enough to reply.

You do that by saying something playful, slightly unfinished, or unexpected.

Something that makes her think, "What does he mean by that?"

For example:

> "ur cute but you look like trouble"

Or:

> "hey [name], you know what's interesting about your pictures?"

Both messages give her something to react to.

She can ask what you mean.

She can disagree.

She can play along.

Either way, she has opened the conversation for you.

You are not trying to impress her with the perfect line. You are just giving her a reason to send the first real reply.

## What To Avoid

Do not open with a boring question she has already answered ten times that day.

Do not send a huge paragraph because you are worried about getting it wrong.

Do not turn sexual straight away.

Do not send a curiosity hook if you have nothing to say when she asks what you mean.

The goal is not to trick her into replying.

The goal is to start the conversation with a bit of curiosity, flirtiness, and momentum.

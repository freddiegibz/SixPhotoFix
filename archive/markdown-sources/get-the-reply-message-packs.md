# Get The Reply: Messages To Use

Pick one of the messages below and send it. If you are using the fill-in-the-blanks, take a real detail from her profile and use the examples for inspiration.

## Fill In The Blanks

When there is something in her profile to work with, these are the stronger openers to use. They make it clear you noticed her, not like you sent the same line to every match. Replace everything in brackets before you send it.

### How To Fill One In

First, find one clear detail in her profile. Use something you can actually see or read: a photo, a prompt answer, a dog, a trip, a hobby, or a drink she mentioned.

Then turn that detail into a light assumption. You are not trying to describe her perfectly. You are giving her something playful to agree with, deny, or explain.

For this template:

> okay, the [photo / prompt / detail] tells me you are either [playful assumption] or [playful assumption]. which one is it?

If her profile has a photo of her jumping into the sea, fill it in like this:

> okay, the jumping-in-the-sea photo tells me you are either fun on holiday or an absolute liability. which one is it?

The photo is the real detail. "fun on holiday" and "an absolute liability" are the two playful assumptions. Neither needs to be true. They just give her an easy reason to reply.

> okay, the [photo / prompt / detail] tells me you are either [playful assumption] or [playful assumption]. which one is it?

> i have a theory about the [photo / prompt / detail], but i need you to confirm it first

> that [photo / prompt / detail] is giving me [playful assumption] energy. fair?

> i cannot work out whether the [photo / prompt / detail] makes you look more [positive trait] or more [playful trait]

> be honest, was the [photo / prompt / detail] your idea or did someone make you do it?

> i feel like there is a story behind the [photo / prompt /detail]. how wrong am i?

> the [photo / prompt / detail] is a very bold choice. i respect it

> you seem like the kind of person who would [playful assumption based on profile detail]. tell me i am wrong

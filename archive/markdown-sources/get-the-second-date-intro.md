# 8. Get The Second Date

The date is over. You had a good time. Now do not disappear for three days trying to look cool.

If you enjoyed seeing her, let her know.

You do not need a huge message about how amazing she is. You do not need to work out whether she is definitely the one. A second date is simply your chance to see her again and find out more.

Start with one short message that says you had a good time and mentions something real from the date.

> made it home. had a good time tonight. still laughing about [thing from the date]

Send it that night if the date clearly went well, or the next day if you are not completely sure.

If she replies warmly, suggest a second plan while the date is still fresh.

## What Not To Do

### Do Not Wait Three Days

Waiting does not make you look more confident. It makes her wonder whether you actually enjoyed the date.

If you had a good time, send a normal message that night or the next day.

### Do Not Send A Huge Emotional Message

You have only been on one date. You do not need to tell her she is different from everyone else, that you cannot stop thinking about her, or that you have never felt this way before.

Keep it warm and real. One specific moment from the date is stronger than a paragraph of compliments.

### Do Not Say "We Should Do This Again Sometime"

That sounds nice, but it is vague.

If you want to see her again, suggest something you could actually do together next week.

### Do Not Pretend The Date Did Not Happen

"hey, how is your day going?" the next morning makes it sound as though you are starting a new conversation with someone you barely know.

Reference the date. Let her know you were present and enjoyed it.

### Do Not Chase A Lukewarm Reply

If she gives you a short answer, takes a long time to reply, or does not help keep the conversation going, do not try to convince her into a second date.

You made your interest clear. Let her meet you there.

# Get The Second Date: Messages To Use

## If The Date Clearly Went Well

Send one of these once you are both home, or the next morning.

> made it home. had a really good time tonight. still laughing about [thing from the date]

> i had a good time tonight. [specific moment / story] is still making me laugh

> home safe? had a great time with you tonight

> that was fun. i am still thinking about [specific food / joke / story from the date]

## If The Date Was Good But You Are Not Completely Sure

Keep it warm without overselling it.

> had a good time last night. hope you made it home alright

> good meeting you last night. [specific thing] was a strong highlight

> i enjoyed last night. hope your [thing she mentioned after the date] went well today

## If She Replies Warmly

She says:

> i had a great time too

Now suggest the second date.

> good, because i still think we need to [second-date idea connected to the first date]. are you free next week?

> glad you said that. you mentioned [thing she likes]. let's do that next week

> good. i feel like we need round two. [specific plan] this week?

## If You Mentioned A Second-Date Idea On The First Date

Use that. It feels natural because you are picking up a conversation you already had.

> you mentioned you have never been to [place]. let's fix that next week

> i am still holding you to that [activity / food / place] recommendation. when are you free next week?

> we never settled the [debate]. i think we need a rematch over [simple date idea]

## If She Texts You First

Her:

> had such a good time tonight

You:

> i had a great time too. [specific moment] was brilliant. let's do [second-date idea] next week

## If She Replies, But It Is Lukewarm

Her:

> thanks, had fun

You:

> glad you had a good time

Leave it there.

Do not immediately ask her out again when she is giving you very little back. If she wants to keep it going, she will give you something to work with.

## If She Does Not Reply

Do not send another post-date message straight away.

You sent a clear, kind message. Give her space. If she wants to see you again, she will respond.

## Second-Date Ideas That Feel Natural

Use something connected to what you spoke about on the first date.

> you clearly have strong opinions about [food]. let's test them at [place] next week

> you made [activity] sound far more fun than it should be. come and show me next week

> you still owe me proof that you are actually good at [thing she claimed]. [activity] this week?

> you said you love [place / type of thing]. i know a good one. let's go next week

# 6. Make Sure She Shows Up

The date is in the calendar.

Now there is a temptation to keep texting her every day so the interest does not disappear before you meet.

You do not need to do that.

You already did the important part. You got the yes and made a real plan.

From here, keep things easy. Let the date happen. Then send one normal confirmation close to the time so neither of you is wondering whether the plan is still on.

You cannot force someone to show up. This section is about making the plan clear, handling any changes without making things awkward, and not chasing when someone is not helping you make the date happen.

## What To Do Now

If the date is a few days away, you can keep texting normally if a natural conversation happens. You do not need to manufacture one every day.

The day before the date, or the morning of an evening date, send one quick confirmation.

> still good for [place] at [time] tomorrow?

That is all you need. It is not needy. You are confirming a plan that involves both of you giving up part of your day.

If she says yes, great. You are set.

If she needs to rearrange and gives you another day, make the new plan.

If she cancels without offering another time, leave the next move with her.

## What Not To Do

### Do Not Try To Keep The Date Alive With Constant Texting

You do not need to spend the next three days finding new things to say just because a date is booked.

Too much texting can burn through the conversation before you have met. Save some of it for in person.

### Do Not Send Three Confirmations

One confirmation message is enough.

"Still on?" followed by "hello?" followed by "are we doing this?" makes a simple plan feel stressful.

### Do Not Act Annoyed If She Needs To Rearrange

Plans change. If she apologises and offers another day, keep it easy and book the new time.

### Do Not Do All The Rearranging For Her

If she cancels and does not suggest another day, do not start listing every night you are free.

Say something simple, leave the door open, and let her show whether she actually wants to meet.

### Do Not Turn Up Without A Clear Confirmation

If you have sent the confirmation and she has not replied, do not spend your evening travelling across town hoping she appears.

Wait for a clear yes before you set off.

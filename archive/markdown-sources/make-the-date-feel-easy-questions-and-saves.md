# Make The Date Feel Easy: Questions And Saves

You do not need to use all of these. Keep a few in the back of your mind, then use the one that fits what you are already talking about.

After she answers, react to it. Tell her your answer too. That is what stops the question becoming an interview.

## Things She Actually Enjoys

> what do you love doing when you have a completely free day?

> what is something you could talk about for far too long if someone let you?

> what have you got into recently that you did not expect to enjoy?

## Travel And Good Days Out

> where would you go back to tomorrow if you could?

> what is the best trip you have ever been on, and what made it good?

> are you a city-break person, beach person, or disappearing somewhere with no phone signal person?

## Food, Drinks, Music, And Things You Have Opinions On

> what is a food everyone loves that you think is massively overrated?

> what is your very specific comfort meal?

> what song will get you to the dance floor even if you swear you are not dancing?

> what is the worst drink you have ever pretended to like?

## Growing Up And The People Around You

> were you the sensible one or the problem child when you were younger?

> what were you weirdly obsessed with as a kid?

> who in your family are you most like?

## Work Without Making It Boring

> what is the bit of your job you actually enjoy?

> what would you do if money was not part of the equation?

> what is the funniest thing about working in [her field] that people outside it would never know?

## Playful What-Ifs

> if you could time-travel for one day, where are you going?

> if you had to pick one city to live in for a year, where would it be?

> what would your very niche specialist subject be on a quiz show?

## When The Conversation Goes Quiet

Try one of these after you have given the silence a second to breathe:

> okay, we have hit the bit where both people pretend they are not trying to think of the next question

> i need a better topic. what is your most controversial opinion about [food / music / your city]?

> wait, go back to [thing she mentioned]. i want the full story

> this is a very important compatibility question. [choose a light topic]

> i feel like you have an unexpectedly strong opinion about something. what is it?

## Topics To Handle Carefully

If these come up naturally, you can talk about them. Just do not force them into the first hour.

- Your exes and old relationship drama
- Deep personal trauma
- Rants about dating apps
- Salary, debt, or interrogating her finances
- Marriage, children, and the full future of the relationship

The first date is for getting to know the person in front of you. You do not need to work out your whole future together over one drink.

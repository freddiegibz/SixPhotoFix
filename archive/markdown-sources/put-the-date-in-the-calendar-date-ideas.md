# Put The Date In The Calendar: Date Ideas To Use

You have got the yes. Now turn it into an actual plan she can say yes to.

Below are date ideas for different times, budgets, weather and moods, with messages to send for each one. Pick the idea that fits the conversation you have already had, then give her a day, time and place. The best first-date plans are simple enough to agree to quickly, but give you somewhere to go next if the date is going well.

## The Easy Default: A Drink Somewhere You Can Talk

Best for: weekday evenings, a chat with good energy, and someone who drinks.

Pick a relaxed bar, pub, wine bar, or cocktail place where you can actually hear each other.

> thursday at 7 work for a drink at [bar name]?

> you have convinced me we need to continue this debate in person. drink at [bar name] on saturday?

## Coffee And A Walk

Best for: daytime, a lower budget, good weather, or someone who does not drink.

Choose a coffee place near a park, market, waterfront, or nice area to walk around. The coffee gets you started. The walk gives you a natural second part if you are getting on.

> coffee at [cafe name] on saturday afternoon? we can go for a walk after if the weather behaves

> sunday coffee at [cafe name]? there is a decent walk nearby if we do not hate each other after twenty minutes

## Dessert Or Ice Cream

Best for: a light evening plan, warmer weather, or when a drink feels too standard.

It is short, cheap enough, and gives you something to do with your hands while you talk.

> you seem like someone with strong dessert opinions. let's test that at [place] this week

> ice cream at [place] on sunday? you can tell me why your flavour choice is objectively better than mine

## A Food Market Or Street-Food Spot

Best for: weekends, daytime, and someone who clearly likes food or trying new places.

It gives you things to react to, keeps the pressure off sitting across a table for two hours, and lets the date stay short or run longer naturally.

> [market name] this saturday? you can show me whether your food recommendations are actually any good

> there is a [food market] on sunday. want to go and find the best thing there?

## A Bookshop, Gallery, Or Museum With Coffee After

Best for: rain, quieter personalities, or when her profile gives you a genuine reason to suggest it.

Do not choose this just to sound cultured. Choose it if you would actually enjoy it and it fits something you have talked about.

> you have made me curious about [topic]. want to check out [gallery / museum / bookshop] this weekend and get coffee after?

> [exhibition] is on at [place]. it looks like your kind of thing. saturday afternoon?

## Mini Golf, Darts, Bowling, Or An Arcade

Best for: a playful chat, a rainy evening, or when you both seem up for a little competition.

The activity gives you something to laugh about, but make sure there is still room to talk. Pick one short activity, not an all-night tournament.

> you seem suspiciously competitive. mini golf at [place] this week?

> i need to know whether you are actually good at bowling or just confident about it. [place] on thursday?

## A Simple Plan For Bad Weather

If it is raining, do not panic and reinvent the date.

Use a quiet bar, cafe, dessert place, food hall, gallery, bookshop, mini golf, darts, bowling, or an arcade.

> weather has ruined the original plan, so let's do [indoor place] at [time] instead

## A Simple Plan When Money Is Tight

You do not need to spend loads to show someone a good time.

Coffee, a walk, ice cream, a market, a free gallery, a bookshop, or a cheap drink all work. Pick something you can comfortably pay for and enjoy.

> coffee at [place] on sunday? there is a free gallery around the corner if we are getting on

## Before You Send The Plan

- [ ] Is it public and easy to get to?
- [ ] Can you actually talk there?
- [ ] Is it simple enough for a first meeting?
- [ ] Have you named a day, time, and place?
- [ ] Would you genuinely enjoy this date yourself?

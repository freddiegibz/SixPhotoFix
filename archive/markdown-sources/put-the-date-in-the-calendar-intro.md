# 5. Put The Date In The Calendar

She has said yes.

Good. Now turn that yes into an actual plan before it turns into "we should meet sometime" and disappears into the chat.

You do not need to plan the perfect date. You need to pick a simple place where you can talk, suggest a day and time, and make it easy for her to say yes or offer another option.

For a first date, simple is good.

You are meeting someone you have only spoken to on an app. The point is to see whether you get on in person, not to impress her with an expensive night or an over-planned itinerary.

## Start With One Clear Plan

Pick one idea from the date ideas page.

Then send her:

> [day] at [time] work for [date idea] at [place]?

For example:

> thursday at 7 work for a drink at [bar name]?

That gives her a real plan to answer. She can say yes, suggest another time, or tell you what does not work.

If she suggests a different day, that is fine. Confirm it and keep moving.

## What A Good First-Date Plan Has

Choose somewhere that is:

- Public and easy for both of you to get to
- Quiet enough to talk without shouting
- Affordable enough that it does not feel like a big production
- Short enough to leave after an hour if there is no connection
- Easy to extend if you are both having a good time

A coffee near a good walk, a drink in a relaxed bar, or dessert somewhere with other places nearby all work because they have a natural beginning and an easy next step.

## What Not To Do

### Do Not Leave It As "Sometime"

"We should meet sometime" feels positive, but it is not a plan.

Once she has said yes, offer a day, a time, and a place. That is how the date becomes real.

### Do Not Make Her Plan Everything

"What do you want to do?" sounds easy, but it gives her another decision to make before you have even met.

Pick something simple. She can always suggest a change if she wants to.

### Do Not Make The First Date A Huge Event

Dinner at an expensive restaurant, a full-day activity, or a plan that takes hours can feel like a lot for two people who have not met yet.

Start with something easy. If you are getting on, you can keep walking, get another drink, or plan a bigger second date.

### Do Not Pick Somewhere You Cannot Talk

A loud club, cinema, or big group event gives you very little space to actually get to know each other.

Choose somewhere you can talk naturally, with something around you to react to if the conversation needs help.

### Do Not Pick Somewhere Isolated

Meet somewhere public. It is more comfortable for both of you, especially when you have only known each other through an app.
